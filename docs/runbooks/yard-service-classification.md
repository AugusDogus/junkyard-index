# Yard service classification

Reviewed 2026-11-18 against `origin/main` at `8d5c446e`. This is a census of source/location identities, not a deduplicated count of businesses. It covers all 94 registered sources, including the retired Nebraska and Brandywine feeds. Only configured or admitted yards are included; marketplace mirrors, rejected suppliers and unrelated branches are excluded.

`self_service` means customers remove parts from the ingested donors. `full_service` means staff removes or dismantles donor parts for customers, including parts held in shelved inventory. `both` requires customer pulling and staff-supplied parts at the same yard and inventory. Paid parts-pulling from the same lot counts as both. Neighboring full-service yards and unrelated inventories do not. `unknown` is deliberate when the reviewed evidence cannot establish this distinction.

High confidence means an explicit service statement, customer-pulling instructions, staff dismantling/storage evidence, or the heavy-truck salvage rule. Medium confidence means operational evidence or a committed qualification supports the classification without an explicit service label. Low confidence is reserved for unknown. Individually sold parts with prices, grades, “Buy or View Part”, “Call For Price”, or shipping establish full service. An own-site or storefront name explicitly saying U Pull or self-service establishes self service. When both signals refer to the same yard’s donor inventory, classify it as both. Provider software and donor photographs alone do not establish either service model. Parts-sale evidence without an explicit service statement has medium confidence.

Committed configurations, runbooks and native fixtures were reviewed first. Live owner pages and public directories were read serially with a normal browser User-Agent, at least three seconds apart (including redirects). HTTP 403 stopped requests to Big D, Paradise, eBay and Yelp hosts; no bypass was attempted. TLS/DNS failures were not bypassed. Local captures and request receipts are retained under `.scratchpad/yard-service/`, outside the deliverable. Evidence below includes the relevant statement or description and a committed reference or owner URL.

The runtime module uses defaults for uniform chains and single-yard sources, exact location overrides for mixed providers, and an unknown fallback for unreviewed mixed-provider locations. A new chain location inherits the reviewed chain rule. A new independent tenant requires its own review. No DB, search index or UI behavior is changed.
