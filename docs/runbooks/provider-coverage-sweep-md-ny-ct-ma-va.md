## Scope and evidence

The [CSV](provider-coverage-sweep-md-ny-ct-ma-va.csv) retains 1,040 directory records with the requested eight columns. It preserves aliases and unresolved listings so subsequent sweeps can finish them. A live business screen means that the business URL was requested, including failed connections and denied hosts. The 565 live website counts deduplicate `www` aliases within a state. They do not count the 53 inherited screens, directory-only aliases, or unlocated rows as newly inspected websites. A shared company site can appear in more than one state without establishing donor attribution in either state. Counts are not 1,040 independent inventory censuses.

`inventory=y` means a public per-vehicle surface was identified, including existing providers and unqualified surfaces. `inventory=n` means none was verified by the recorded evidence; blocked and unlocated sites remain unknown. Only `result=qualified` adds a provider. Blank cities were not guessed. Generic homepage screening can miss unlinked catalogs, and unresolved/blocked entries remain follow-up work. This is a broad discovered-business list, not proof that every operating yard in these states was found or inspected.

Discovery used:

- Car-Part's state recycler directories for MD, NY, CT, MA and VA, using yard names and business links only, never Car-Part parts inventory for qualification.
- ARA's public member directory API for all five states, ARANY member listings, VARA's full-member list, and Automotive Recyclers of Massachusetts. ARM's member map publishes `/cgi-bin/memjson?pass=pass`, which supplied 42 directory entries. Connecticut's association site did not connect.
- Statewide Maps business searches, additional Syracuse/Rochester/Buffalo/Albany and heavy-truck searches, and named business markers. Search/Yelp results and SalvageAtlas's searchable-parts directory supplemented these. Atlas supplied 135 rows across MD, NY, MA and VA; its CT route did not resolve.
- The supplied November 17 tally, November 16 discovery rejections, `docs/provider-candidates.md`, and recent saved regional investigations. Previous decisions based only on optional fields were not used as mandatory blockers.

Research bodies, request logs and intermediate censuses remain in `.scratchpad/provider-coverage/sweep-md-ny-ct-ma-va/`. Key files are `census.json`, `supplemental-census.json`, `supplemental-directory.json`, `atlas-directory.json`, `final-census.json`, `requests.jsonl`, `attempts.jsonl` and `runbook-summary.json`. CSV reasons name individual captures. Duda JSON responses were decoded to inspect their actual content and navigation instead of treating CSS identifiers as VINs.

The separate APS/URG-directory sweep owns supplier codes in the parent's `aps-member-census/screening.json`. Those suppliers were excluded from further inventory acquisition after the coordination update. Earlier Nationwide EE23 screening is retained as evidence but claims no credit here. BC Automotive and Aesop members without independent complete sites remain excluded. Copart, IAA and Car-Part are not providers. Existing business families and marketplaces do not receive another credit for aliases or additional yards.

## Requests and normal-run cost

Paradise adds **two inventory HTTP requests per seeded run**, one opening card POST and one independent closing POST. Decoding its 182 makes without its committed seed needs four additional vPIC POSTs. The full configured Hollander source now has 52 yards, 57,260 rows, 56,446 accepted donors and 15,640 unique missing-make VINs. It uses **104 inventory requests plus one vPIC POST**, or **105 HTTP requests before retries**, with the committed seed and the existing unresolved VIN still uncached. There are 313 logical decode batches; cached batches advance without HTTP. A completely uncached run would need 417 requests. Existing row, body, cursor, lifetime, rejection and duplicate guards are unchanged.

Discovery recorded **882 completion receipts: 698 HTTP responses and 184 transport errors**, including redirects, directory acquisition, screening and qualification. Of the HTTP responses, 412 were status 200. Three additional response bodies were saved before a malformed placeholder telephone URL caused link extraction to fail (Mark's two roots and arrivals); those were manually inspected and the helper fixed. Thus the direct acquisition count is at least 885 attempts. Older automatically followed redirects and missing pre-send records prevent an exact total wire-request claim. Separately, 31 saved search/Maps job results include failed search-engine tasks; they are not 31 successful business inspections. Ten selected successful Paradise qualification responses are committed, nine direct provider/vPIC responses and one Maps result. They are an evidence subset, not the whole discovery cost.

Business requests use a normal Chrome browser User-Agent. Initially the generic scanner incorrectly held the shared URG gate for roots, processing and sleeps. After the parent's coordination update, it was corrected: generic requests serialize and wait 3.5 seconds per host, while known URG inventory requests hold `flock /tmp/jyi-urg-gate.lock` only across HTTP request/body acquisition. Pacing, parsing and writing occur after releasing the shared gate. Assigned supplier codes are skipped. A 403 or 429 stops that host while other hosts continue. No CAPTCHA was defeated. Captures stay under the worktree, never `/tmp`.
