# OEM Auto Recyclers

See the current [source flags and census floors](provider-coverage-go-live.md).

The `oem` source covers the OH40 operation at 481 Township Road 27, Bluffton OH 45817. Native ownership, full-service inventory and two VIN-matching donor destinations bind the catalog to OEM Auto Recyclers. Partner parts sourcing adds no provider or yard credit. The saved published-yard roster and local qualified register have no matching operator, address or phone.

The November 15 UTC census follows 45 makes, 342 model controls and 60 arrivals. All 24 homepage-carousel stocks occur in arrivals. The alternate published arrivals page has the same 60 ordered records. The complete union has 2,227 observations: 2,158 accepted donors, 43 rejects or quarantines and 26 duplicate extras. Three conflicting VIN groups remain quarantined. Five valid rejected VINs remain presence evidence for reconciliation. Historical: all 152 closing controls of that capture passed under the closing design of the time, which re-read every directory and the first, last and badge-mismatched model pages. Current closing is described below.

The yard point comes from a Census Public_AR_Current exact-address match. It is a street-range interpolation, not rooftop or parcel verification. Native Google viewport coordinates are not used. Actual geocode bytes and the receipt are in `.scratchpad/provider-coverage/nov14-provider-goal/research/ga_fl_al/refill04/oem-census-geocode.*`.

Model pages follow the shared [model census](model-census.md) with `reuse: "never"`: every run reads every published model page. Badge-only reuse was removed because a donor crushed and another placed under the same model keeps the badge, so a carried page would miss the swap. The census still supplies read reasons (`cold` and `gap` on full reads; otherwise `new`, `count`, `trigger`, `short` or `daily`), short-page confirmation in closing, a 97% replay floor over every model with a last accepted page, and the badge-only outage path once the last accepted run is 6 days old. OEM specifics: an 82% badge floor (badges overstate cards because model pages lag arrivals), a closing pass that confirms short pages, and one connector trigger label. A current arrival never labels a read; a model's read is labelled `trigger` on the day an arrival donor bound to it leaves the arrivals page while missing from its last accepted page. The census contract is `oem/OH40/literal-model-graph/arrival-union/make-directory-closing/incremental-model-census-v4-expected-surplus-replay`.

Requests are serial, with a 1.6-second completed gap, no retries, a 40-second request/body deadline and eight GETs per three-minute chunk. Bodies are limited to 4 MiB. Serialized cursors are limited to 8 MiB, room for the previous census plus this run's pages (about 1.8 MiB each). Closing repeats the make directory and every model page read this run whose card count differs from its badge or falls below its expected cards (its badge plus a sticky surplus of placeholder or surplus rows, see [Incremental model census](model-census.md)); a changed directory or card list stops emission. A re-read matches when it lists the same VIN-bearing cards (card ID, link, stock, year, title and VIN) in any order and the same number of VIN-less cards: the site re-sorts cards of the same year and edits VIN-less placeholders (such as stock FBM) between reads, and neither reaches the snapshot. A page below its badge is stored as confirmed only when its closing re-read matched in a normal incremental run (an accepted census from the same day or the day before); cold, gap and outage full reads confirm none, so the next run reads their short pages as `short` again and closing re-reads them (see [Incremental model census](model-census.md)); explicit empties under nonzero badges stay confirmed while they keep matching. Any other page below its expected cards is read as `short` on the next run. Before anything emits, read model cards, each capped at its badge, must cover at least 82% of today's model badges. Badges overstate cards because model pages lag arrivals: the November 15 census covered 2,167 of 2,428 (89.25%; 23 explicit-empty models hold 34 badge units and 80 partial pages miss 227). Emptying any sixth of its model pages (one of the six former sweep slices) drops that to 70.5% to 79.0%, so a fault that breaks that many pages fails that run. While the last accepted run is under 6 days old, read cards must also cover at least 97% of the last accepted card counts of every model with a last accepted page, each reference lowered by any drop in that model's badge. Cutting one such sixth to a single card per model leaves 73.0% to 80.6% of badges (lightest: former slice 5, 51 models, 1,957 of 2,428) and 81.8% to 90.3% of the last accepted cards (lightest: 1,957 of 2,167), so the replay floor fails every such cut even where badge lag would hide it. Inventory emits only after complete closing and quality checks. Durable validation requires terminal cursor evidence and retains the 719-donor minimum, 10% rejection, 25% duplicate and 50% previous-count drift guards. Optional stock, photos, color, dates and position remain nullable.
