# HeavyTruckParts.net salvage suppliers

See the current [source flags and census floors](provider-coverage-go-live.md).

`heavytruckparts` runs unless `ingestion-source-heavytruckparts` serves false. It adds one marketplace credit in each of five states (MA, KY, TN, GA, AZ); SC and FL receive no credit. Dutchers (NY) and C&S (SD) retain their existing source keys, behavior and enumeration policies. No enablement, deployment or merge is included.

A salvage or dismantling yard's Complete Vehicle and Vehicle For Sale listings may qualify. Explicit retail stock, equipment, attachments, trailers, boats and golf carts do not. Numeric prices, mileage sales copy (including Please Call stock) and running/ready-to-work sales language are excluded unless the listing explicitly establishes salvage or non-running status. This deliberately withholds ambiguous priced stock. Exclusions remain visible in raw accounting and are separate from identity rejects.

Rydemore describes a demanufacturing facility; only eight non-running or incomplete units survive the retail filter. West Side's mixed roster includes passenger vehicles and motorcycles as well as trucks. CAT CT660 road trucks remain eligible; CAT loaders do not. All County loses one priced retail truck and has one benign duplicate. Crest explicitly specializes in late-model salvage and lists dismantled donors. Its item 10191, advertised as "TEST DRVN RUNS GREAT ... SUPER NICE," is excluded. Item 11549 shares that VIN but explicitly describes a dismantled donor, so it remains eligible; this reduces duplicates rather than accepted vehicles. American describes separate dealership and parts operations: only items 542292, 542296 and 542297, explicitly marked “This truck can not be sold complete,” qualify. Its generic USED listings do not establish salvage scope.

Required identity is a native VIN or pre-1981 manufacturer serial, year, make and model. Modern VINs also pass the standard check digit. Blank/placeholder identity is rejected without deriving make/model from a VIN or description. Each accepted record links to its supplier/store/category-bound native item. Optional stock, arrival, color and yard position stay null; vendor logos become null images. Rejected format-valid identities protect observed presence. Conflicting duplicate claims or cross-yard VIN ownership stop the entire source.

## Enumeration and failure behavior

The generic source uses per-supplier ID, store, item category, native owner/geography, published address and yard coordinates. It reuses the existing shared HTP HTTP client and card parser. The new cursor graph imports only pure schemas, metadata and census validation. HTTP, HTML parsing and Effect stay outside its Workflow import graph.

For each configured supplier, read the opening independent store count, every descending-year page, every ascending-year page, then the closing store count. Require exact native Next/Last controls, page sizes, item ownership, item metadata agreement across repeated IDs, equal per-year counts and a union matching the independent store total. Repeated destinations across sort pages are traversal overlap; distinct items sharing one consistent VIN are source duplicates. No vehicles, yards, presence or accounting emit until all five suppliers close and validate.

A normal unchanged census costs 104 serial GETs. Checkpoints bind supplier order, scope/config, prior accepted count, request/byte accounting and the exact page frontier. Terminal resume fetches and emits nothing. Each chunk admits at most eight physical GETs, pauses after 60 seconds between requests and has a four-minute deadline. Whole runs stop at 512 requests or one hour. Responses are limited to 2 MiB and checkpoints to 8 MiB. Pages declare UTF-8 but carry stray windows-1252 bytes (0xD7 on every Crest page, 0x92 on some American cards), so HTP passes U+FFFD replacement to the shared heavytruckparts.net client. Dutcher, C&S and every other source stay strict, and offline replays decode the saved bytes the same way.

Per-supplier minimum accepted counts are 2/188/13/72/1 in the table's order. Each supplier independently enforces the unchanged 10% rejection and 25% duplicate ceilings, excluding intentional out-of-scope rows from those denominators. Whole-source validation requires at least 277 unique vehicles (unchanged when CA Truck Parts left the roster) and the existing 50% prior-run drift guard. An invalid count, drift, malformed page, ownership change, block or quality failure preserves previous inventory and the last committed checkpoint. Inspect saved evidence before restarting; do not raise a guard to admit bad data.

Catalog turnover during the census (`HtpCensusDrift`) is a transient rejection: a page that holds fewer or more items than the store count, Next/Last controls that disagree with it, a changed closing store count, per-year counts that differ between the two year orders, a year that lists more items after its fills than the year orders counted, or a repeated item whose details changed between reads. On November 18, West Side's last page was short of its opening count. The source keeps its prior inventory and retries with a fresh census after the usual 2-hour delay, within its 3 daily attempts; after the last attempt it runs again the next day. Evidence that does not fit turnover stays deterministic: a Next or Last control that is not exactly one link to another page of the same query, a page 1 or make page with no items, or a year that still lists fewer items than counted after its fills. Ownership, structure, identity and quality failures also stay deterministic and end the day.

HTP robots.txt publishes Crawl-delay 4 and disallows `page=` URLs. Explicit native pagination is used for these count-checked full catalogs, as in Dutchers: West Side alone spans 25 pages and requires a complete unfiltered roster, including older and malformed-year rows that year-range controls cannot prove. Every deployed HTP client, including Dutchers and C&S, takes the same HTP host lease. The daily pass starts one of the three sources at a time and the next once it has fetched (`HostClaims`, see [crawl pacing without Redis](../design/crawl-pacing-without-redis.md)), so the lease is kept in the process. It covers complete response bodies and four completed seconds, including failures. A busy host waits at most five minutes; store errors, deadlines or lost lease ownership fail the chunk. Local tools and tests retain the process-wide semaphore. Requests have no retries, manual redirects, a 25-second response deadline and a separate 25-second body deadline. All three sources also retain the same process-wide semaphore.

## Evidence and diagnostics

Qualification made **174 live requests: 157 HTP GETs and 17 geocoder GETs**, all serial. Every HTP response was HTTP 200. Request receipts prove a minimum 4.200-second completed gap across all requests, including the production-client store smoke check. No ordinary HTTP filtering changes were necessary. Two initial Nominatim requests returned 429; later identifying-User-Agent structured address requests succeeded. These failures are retained.

Three admitted coordinates are unique Census Public_AR_Current address-range interpolations, not rooftop points. Crest and American use unique OpenStreetMap Nominatim house-address matches (ways 546691786 and 274202609). Census did not match Crest. A broadened Census Phoenix query incorrectly returned **East** Broadway, so that point was discarded; Nominatim matched 2850 **West** Broadway Road and ZIP 85041. The saved responses ground every admitted coordinate.

`src/server/ingestion/heavytruckparts-test-fixtures/native.json.gz` is a lossless base64 line dictionary plus per-response line indexes. It preserves original response bytes, including shared scripts and styles, without repeating page chrome. `receipts.json` records every URL, status, final URL, byte count and request timing. Offline tests reconstruct the captured responses and never contact the network.

```sh
bun test src/server/ingestion/heavytruckparts-connector.test.ts src/server/ingestion/cstruck-client.test.ts
bun run soak:sources -- --sources=heavytruckparts --cycles=1 --max-pages=104
```

The soak is a live, count-only diagnostic. It discards vehicles, uses the same pacing and preserves an incomplete checked cursor at its physical-GET cap. Do not run it in offline tests.

Local verification: `bun run lint`, `bun run typecheck`, changed-file oxfmt, and `bun test --timeout 15000 src`. Shared-host tests use a fake atomic store with independent instances, HTTP errors, cancellation, timeouts and store failures. Retail and make tests replay original saved rows. No new live HTP requests were made for these fixes.
